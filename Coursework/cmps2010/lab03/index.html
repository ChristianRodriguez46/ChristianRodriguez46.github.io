CMPS 2010 · Lab 3

Guessing game and calculator

A number guessing game and a small calculator. The game picks a random number, prints three hints about it (sign, even or odd, and whether it is between -4 and 4) and allows one guess. The calculator reads two integers and an operator and applies it with a switch.

View code on GitHubREADME

What I did

  • Seeded rand with srand(time(0)) and scaled it into the range -10 to 10 in lab3-1.cpp and -20 to 20 in 3lab-2.cpp.
  • Printed the hints with if/else checks on the sign, num % 2 and the -4 to 4 range.
  • Reworked the ending in 3lab-2.cpp so the joke "another hint?" question only comes after a wrong guess.
  • Wrote the calculator in lab3-2.cpp as a switch on the operator character, with a default case that rejects anything other than + - * / %.

How to build and run

From the folder with the code:

g++ -o lab3-1 lab3-1.cpp
./lab3-1

g++ -o lab3-2 lab3-2.cpp
./lab3-2

Requires: A C++ compiler such as g++ (Linux, macOS, or WSL on Windows).
