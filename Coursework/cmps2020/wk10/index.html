CMPS 2020 · Week 10

Deque and a window manager

Doubly linked structures. Lab 12 writes a deque and derives a Queue and a Stack from it. Lab 13 is a window switcher that keeps open apps in a circular doubly linked list with a dummy node and moves between them from a menu.

View code on GitHubREADME

What I did

  • Wrote the deque's addTohead, addTotail, removeFromhead and removeFromtail, keeping the next and prev links and the head and tail pointers in step.
  • Derived Queue (add at the head, remove from the tail) and Stack (add and remove at the head), returning *this so calls chain, as in q.enqueue("Heaven").enqueue("Hell").
  • Wrote startApp, findApp, closeApp, next and previous for lab 13, skipping the dummy node when moving around the circle.
  • Filled in the menu actions in main for launching, closing, finding and switching apps.

How to build and run

From the folder with the code:

g++ -o lab12 lab12_2020TH_ChrRod.cpp
./lab12

g++ -o lab13 lab13_2020TH_ChrRod.cpp
./lab13

Requires: A C++ compiler such as g++ (Linux, macOS, or WSL on Windows).
