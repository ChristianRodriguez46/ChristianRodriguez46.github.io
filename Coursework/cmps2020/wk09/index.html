CMPS 2020 · Week 9

Queues and a ring buffer

Two queues. Lab 10 is a print spooler that keeps jobs in a linked list and prints them oldest first. Lab 11 is a ring buffer on a four-slot dynamic array, driven by a menu that enqueues and dequeues names.

View code on GitHubREADME

What I did

  • Wrote enqueue to add each job at the head and dequeue to walk to the tail and remove it, with separate cases for one, two and more nodes.
  • Freed the remaining jobs in deallocate, called from the destructor.
  • Wrote the ring buffer's enqueue, dequeue, isEmpty and isFull, wrapping the front and back indexes with % size.
  • Kept one slot empty to tell a full buffer from an empty one, so four slots hold three names.

How to build and run

From the folder with the code:

./lab10

g++ -o lab11 lab11_2020TH_ChrRod.cpp
./lab11

Requires: A C++ compiler such as g++ (Linux, macOS, or WSL on Windows).
