CMPS 2020 · Week 13

Recursion, sorting and heaps

Recursion, sorting and heaps. Lab 15 prints and frees the browser history stack with recursive functions, lab 16 times bubble, insertion and quick sort, and lab 17 keeps an array as a max-heap and heap sorts it.

View code on GitHubREADME

What I did

  • Replaced the history stack's loops with recursion in lab 15: shownode prints a node and recurses on next, and deallocate recurses to the end of the list before deleting.
  • Wrote bubble_sort and insertion_sort and timed every sort with std::chrono on random, increasing and decreasing data. fill_array_dec writes size - 1 into every slot, so the "decreasing" runs actually sort identical values.
  • Wrote a quick_sort with a median-of-three pivot that hands small left partitions to insertion sort. As written it then skips the right partition, so the result is not always fully sorted.
  • Wrote is_max_heap, heapify, build_max_heap and heap_sort for lab 17, rebuilding the heap after every number entered.
