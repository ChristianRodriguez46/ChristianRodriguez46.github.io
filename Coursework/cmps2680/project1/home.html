My Hobbies

Watching Hockey

I began watching hockey when I was around eight years old when I went to see the Bakersfield Condors at the Rabobank Arena, which is now known as Mechanics Bank Arena. I used to go with my dad and siblings as a family activity when I was eight years old. I've been interested in hockey for many years after seeing numerous Condors games while they were in the ECHL, but I was unable to attend as many games for a long period of time. I was excited to be able to attend more Condors games recently, but as I began to watch some games, I was informed that the Condors had moved up a league since they became associated with an NHL team known as the Edmonton Oilers. Even before the Oilers were linked with the Condors, the Oilers were my favorite NHL team. When there are fights and heavy body checks throughout a game, that is what keeps me interested in hockey. In the future, I'd want to see the Condors win a playoff season in Bakersfield. Also, I wish to attend a real NHL game featuring the Edmonton Oilers

Playing Video Games

My interest in video games began as a youngster, when my elder brother introduced me to an early Xbox console. The appeal of vivid worlds and intriguing storytelling drew me in right away, providing a nice break from everyday life. Over a decade later, my love for gaming hasn't waned, spurred by the ongoing growth of gaming technology and the anticipation of new releases. The ever-improving visuals, deep plot, and vast open areas continue to wow me. Furthermore, the social side of gaming, in which I collaborate with friends to complete challenges or compete against opponents, develops a feeling of community within the thriving online gaming community. Looking ahead, I view my gaming interest as more than simply a means to pass the time and escape reality; I want to get involved in the world of eSports and maybe contribute through content production, competitive play, or game development. It is my ambition to use my passion for gaming to connect with other gamers and develop the gaming culture that has provided me so much joy.
