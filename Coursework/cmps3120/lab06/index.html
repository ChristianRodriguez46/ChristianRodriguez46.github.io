CMPS 3120 · Lab 6

Greedy connections with a min-heap

Picks K connections between neighboring points on a line, with no point used twice, so that the total gap length is small. A plain greedy pass with a min-heap is compared with a version that corrects its mistakes: after choosing (l, r) it adds a virtual connection (l-1, r+1) to the heap, whose cost is the price of swapping that gap for the two gaps beside it.

View code on GitHubREADME

What I did

  • Started from the instructor's skeleton (its first line lists the lines to fill in) and filled in the right endpoint of each heap node in initheap.
  • Wrote the step in greedyec that replaces each virtual connection (l, r) with the real connections (l, l+1) and (r-1, r).
  • Saved a run with N = 100 and K = 40 in output.txt: plain greedy costs 376 and the corrected version 374.
