CMPS 3560 · Lab 10

Genetic algorithm

A genetic algorithm in Python for the Max-Ones problem: evolve a 32-bit string until every bit is 1. A population of 25 is bred with tournament selection, one-point crossover and a 1% chance of flipping one bit in each child, and test runs here found the answer in roughly 100 to 900 generations.

View code on GitHubREADME

What I did

  • Wrote random_chromosome, fitness (the number of ones), tournament_selection (best of 3), one_point_crossover and mutate as small typed functions.
  • Wrote the generation loop in genetic_algorithm, which replaces the whole population each generation and stops when a chromosome reaches fitness 32 or after 10,000 generations.
  • Printed every individual and the best fitness for each generation in lab10.py.
  • Made MDlab10.py, a notebook-style copy split into # %% cells with short explanations that prints progress every 50 generations.
