CMPS 3560 · Lab 7

Mamdani fuzzy inference

Extends the lab 6 fuzzy sets into Mamdani inference with three rules about distance and temperature that recommend no water, a bottle or a lot. Each rule's strength scales its output set, the scaled sets are combined with max, and their centroid over 0 to 2 liters is the answer: 1.21 L for 2 km at 80F.

View code on GitHubREADME

What I did

  • Kept the membership functions and tests from lab 6, which the script still prints first.
  • Added the fuzzy sets none, alot, medium and long and redefined short, moderate and hot from the lab's appendix.
  • Wrote mamdani_inference(L, T): rule 1 uses short distance, rule 2 takes the min of medium distance and moderate temperature, and rule 3 takes the max of long distance and hot temperature.
  • Aggregated the rules with max at 21 points from 0 to 2 and defuzzified with a weighted average (the centroid).
