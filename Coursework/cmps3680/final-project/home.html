About Me

At just 18 years old, I'm Seth Wise, the mastermind behind a dynamic mobile car detailing business that's not just about cleanliness but about elevating your vehicle to a whole new level. As the proud owner, I bring a level of passion and commitment that goes beyond the surface-much like my dedication to professional racing in the pro-late model category. From tearing up the track in Bandolero and Legends cars in my younger days, to wining the 2022 South West Pro Late Model championship, to now ensuring every car I touch shines with precision, my journey is a testament to the pursuit of excellence.

What sets my detailing services apart is the same racing spirit that propels me on the track-a commitment to performance and perfection. I understand that your car is not just a means of transport; it's an extension of your identity. When you choose my services, you're not just getting a thorough clean; you're getting an experience crafted by someone who lives and breathes the automotive world. Trust me to bring the same level of precision and attention to detail that has propelled me in my racing career to your vehicle. Join me in the fast lane to automotive excellence; let's make your car the true champion it deserves to be!
