How to play

  1. Each round gives you 5 attempts. Score 3 goals to reach the next round.
  2. Each attempt starts at center ice with the puck. You have 8 seconds to shoot.
  3. A goal is worth 100 points. Fake out the goalie with a deke first and the goal is worth 150.
  4. Some saves leave a rebound. Grab it and shoot again within 1.5 seconds.
  5. The goalie gets better as the rounds go on. The run ends when 3 goals are out of reach.
